# Halaa audience and age-rating answer pack

**Owner-approved classification:** general audience; not specifically directed at children; no blanket 18+ restriction.

**Decision status:** `OWNER_APPROVED` — no further owner decision is required unless the product is intentionally redesigned for children.

## Product rule

Halaa may be used by people of different ages. A person creating an account, purchasing, contracting, operating a business/vendor account or giving legally binding instructions must have the required legal capacity or act with legal-guardian authorization. Guests may receive invitations and RSVP with appropriate guardian involvement where applicable.

## Apple

- `Made for Kids`: **No**.
- Do not manually force an adult rating merely because purchases or UGC exist.
- Answer the current questionnaire truthfully for UGC, messaging, location, purchases and report/block controls; accept the calculated regional ratings.
- Age suitability URL: optional; use the public Terms/Privacy routes if a URL is requested after final wording is published.

## Google Play

- App is not primarily child-directed.
- Contains ads: **No**, based on the audited code.
- UGC/communication: **Yes**, with report, block and moderation.
- Purchases: **Yes**.
- Location: **Yes**, where the user invokes location/event/vendor features.
- Complete the mandatory target-audience checkboxes consistently with the approved classification during Play Console setup. Selecting child age groups can activate additional Families-policy review, so do not select them unless Halaa is intentionally designed and verified for those groups.

## Store-entry stop rule

Do not select “Made for Kids,” children-directed, or under-child-threshold target groups merely to maximize reach. Store availability and intended target audience are different. If the owner intentionally targets young children, pause and implement verified guardian authorization, child-appropriate notices and a Families/SDK review first.
